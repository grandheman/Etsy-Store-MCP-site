Personal Etsy Connector

A tool being developed to help its owner prepare consistent product listings and manage their own Etsy shops through an MCP-compatible assistant.

Intended workflow

  • Prepare and edit draft product listings.
  • Upload product images and digital download files.
  • Review listing information before publishing.
  • Keep shop connections separate as the project develops.

Who it is for

Initial use is limited to the owner and their own shops. This is an internal development project, not a publicly available application or a service accepting seller sign-ups.

Development status

The connector is under development and review. The workflow described here is planned functionality, not a claim of a completed integration or Etsy approval. This website is an information page only.

Access and privacy

Connector development is kept in a separate private repository. This static page contains no shop credentials, customer data, sign-in form, or analytics scripts. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages.
